# Vanilla asset-first orc pilot

Preserve the installed vanilla body, hair and head textures. Only skin tint and small ivory tusk overlays are new. No copied game pixels.

References inspected: [south head](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/File:Male_Average_Normal_south.png), [east head](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/File:Male_Average_Normal_east.png), [HAR Body Addons](https://github.com/erdelf/AlienRaces/wiki/Body-Addons), [General Settings](https://github.com/erdelf/AlienRaces/wiki/General-Settings). Downloading source PNGs failed, so overlay coordinates are visually estimated rather than pixel-registered. Four original 128x128 RGBA layers: south two tusks, east one, west mirrored, north transparent.

The normal average heads are selected for the first alignment test. The five body types are inherited. The addon uses a white base channel and head alignment. Draw size and offsets remain provisional until runtime testing; no composite preview or approval yet.
